# Sales Call Script Template — 12-Step Framework

**Last updated:** 2026-03-29
**Usage:** Reference file for `operations-meeting-prep`. Used to build the personalized sales script section in every meeting prep output.

---

## Step 1: Preparing for the Call

- Be On Time: Have the prospect's number ready and dial exactly when the clock hits the scheduled time.
- Clear Your Mind: Release emotions and judgments. Approach the call with a calm, focused mindset.
- Focus on Diagnosing: Don't focus on closing — you're here to diagnose their problems like a doctor before offering a solution.
- Eliminate Distractions: Silence notifications, close irrelevant tabs, and turn off email alerts.
- Set Up Your Workspace: Clean desk, notepad ready, pen available.
- Review Pre-Call Info: If a survey was completed, have it visible and reviewed.
- Record the Call: Ensure call recording software is running.
- Use Headphones: This frees your hands and improves focus.
- Optimize Environment: Find a quiet space; notify anyone nearby to avoid interruptions.
- Practice Deep Breathing: Take a few deep breaths to center yourself.
- Review Past Wins: Recall successful calls to build confidence.
- Hydrate: Keep water nearby to stay sharp.

**Key Insight:** Your preparation sets the tone for a successful call.

---

## Step 2: Micro Rapport

- Start with Genuine Interest: "Hey [Prospect's Name], how are you today? How has your [time period] been so far?"
- Keep It Casual: Spend the first few minutes on light conversation.
- Build a Connection: Ask about shared experiences or current events when appropriate.
  - "How did you get started in [prospect's industry]?"
  - "What do you enjoy most about your work in [prospect's field]?"
- Transition Smoothly: "Great! Let's jump in if you're ready."

**Key Insight:** People buy from people they like and trust.

---

## Step 3: Agenda & Alpha Frame

- Set Clear Expectations: "Here's the plan: I'll ask some questions to better understand your [business/situation], walk you through how we work, and by the end, you can decide if it's a fit. Sound good?"
- Establish Authority: Speak with confidence — you're leading the call.
- Confirm Understanding: "Does that sound like a plan?"

**Key Insight:** The person asking questions controls the conversation.

---

## Step 4: Establish Primary Motive

- Identify Their 'Why': "What motivated you to put this time aside for this call?"
- Dig Deeper:
  - "When you say [prospect's statement], what do you mean exactly?"
  - "Why do you think this [problem/challenge] exists?"
  - "Have you tried fixing this before? What did you try?"
- Understand the Timeline: "How long has this [problem/situation] existed?"
- Additional Probing:
  - "What made you decide to tackle this [issue/problem] now?"
  - "If you don't solve this, what happens next?"

**Key Insight:** The deeper the motive, the stronger the desire to fix it.

---

## Step 5: Understand Where They're At

- Define the Current Situation:
  - "What do you [sell/offer] in your [business/practice]?"
  - "How do you structure your pricing for [product/service]?"
  - "Who is your ideal [customer/client]?"
  - "What are your key [selling points/differentiators]?"
  - "What [problem/challenge] are you solving for your [customers/clients]?"
- Uncover Their Process: "What's your current [sales/service/delivery] process from [start] to [finish]?"
- Explore Team Dynamics:
  - "Who is responsible for [key function] in your [business/organization]?"
  - "Do you have any [team members] or plan to hire some?"

**Key Insight:** Understanding the current landscape reveals weaknesses.

---

## Step 6: Indirectly Cause Pain

- Question Their Acquisition System:
  - "How are you currently getting [customers/clients]?"
  - "Do you know your cost per [customer/acquisition]?"
  - "Do you have a predictable system to generate [leads/prospects]?"
- Introduce Discomfort:
  - "Are you satisfied with your current [growth/results]?"
  - "How much are you currently making per [month/quarter] from [specific revenue stream]?"
- Deep Pain Exploration:
  - "How does this [challenge/issue] impact your [day-to-day operations/business]?"
  - "If nothing changes, how will this affect your [long-term goals/vision]?"

**Key Insight:** People act to avoid pain more than to gain pleasure.

---

## Step 7: Define Their Goal & Future Pace It

- Establish a Clear Target:
  - "What's your [timeframe] [revenue/growth/outcome] goal?"
  - "How much do you want to [make/achieve] per [time period]?"
- Uncover the Motivation:
  - "Why is that goal important to you?"
  - "How would achieving that change your [business/life]?"
- Stretch Their Vision:
  - "Do you think going beyond that goal is possible?"
  - "What would [doubling/exceeding] that target look like for you?"

**Key Insight:** The clearer the goal, the more urgent the action.

---

## Step 8: Get the Prospect to Admit Defeat

- Make Them Recognize the Need for Help: "So you're [current situation] and want to [goal]. What's stopping you from doing this alone?"
- Alternative Phrasing: "If you haven't fixed [problem] yet, what's been holding you back?"
- Listen for Defeat Statements:
  - "I don't know how." → Knowledge gap.
  - "I want to get there faster." → Speed desire.
  - "I want expert guidance." → Mentorship need.

**Key Insight:** No defeat statement = no sale.

---

## Step 9: Establish Conviction & Deep Emotion

- Challenge the Status Quo: "Why not just continue doing what you're doing?"
- Surface the Emotional Impact: "How is this [struggle/challenge] affecting your [life/business/team]?"
- Set a Timeline for Change: "When do you want to fix this [problem/situation]?"
- Gauge Commitment: "On a scale of 1–10, how committed are you to solving this now?"
- Deep Emotion Exploration: "What does achieving this goal mean for your [family/future/legacy]?"
- Use Reflective Listening: "So what I'm hearing is [summarize their key points]. Is that correct?"

**Key Insight:** Emotions drive decisions — logic justifies them.

---

## Step 10: Statement of Confidence & Permission to Explain

- Reinforce Your Certainty: "Based on what you've told me, I'm confident we can help you with [their specific challenge]."
- Ask for Permission: "Would you like me to explain how we can solve [their problem]?"
- Optional Variation: "Would you like me to share the [strategy/approach] we use to get results like [relevant example]?"

**Key Insight:** Permission-based pitches build trust.

---

## Step 11: State Your Expertise & Transition to the Pitch

- Position Yourself as the Expert: "At [Your Company], we help [target audience] achieve [desired outcome] using our proven [system/method]."
- Insert Trust Elements: "We've been doing this for [X years], worked with [Y clients], and have [Z success stories]."
- Share Screen & Explain Process: Open a presentation or visual aid to walk them through the high-level overview of your services. Highlight past results and case studies. Keep It Simple: Focus on the big-picture process; avoid technical details.
- Ask for Confirmation: "Can you see how this could help you achieve [specific goal we discussed earlier]?"

**Key Insight:** The transition to the pitch should feel natural, supported by authority, proof, and clarity.

---

## Step 12: Leverage Incentive-Based Pricing

- Wait for Price Inquiry: Never bring up price first.
- State the Full Price: "Our [service/product] costs [price] per [time period], with a [fee type] of [amount]."
- Offer Incentive Pricing: "But if you decide to move forward today, we'll [specific incentive]."
- Stay Silent: Let the prospect sit with the offer.

**Key Insight:** Silence triggers decision-making.

---

**Final Thought:** Master this script step-by-step. Practice regularly. Record your calls. Sales mastery isn't about perfect words — it's about deep understanding, emotional connection, and confident delivery.
