### Type 2: Lead Magnet

**Engagement range: 0.6x - 0.8x (lower than stories, but higher conversion)**

**Your Lead Magnet DNA:**
- Give away real systems, not PDFs with fluff
- Show the results first (specific numbers or outcomes)
- Break the "comment X to get it" pattern with humor
- Generous but not desperate

**Structure (The Lead Magnet Giveaway):**
1. HOOK: Specific result + what you're giving away
2. PROOF: What this helped you achieve
3. BREAKDOWN: What's inside (bulleted, specific)
4. USE CASES: How they can use it
5. CTA: Direct link OR playful comment request
6. P.S.: Related question or self-aware joke

**The Self-Aware Twist:**
Instead of: "Comment 'Templates' and I'll send it"
Try: "Comment Templ... I'm kidding, the link is below"

These mechanics create belonging — not recurring phrases.

## Part 6: The Belonging Checklist

Before every post, check:
- [ ] Is there a named character from your universe?
- [ ] Is the struggle visible? (Not just the win)
- [ ] Is there an origin anchor? (Your background, roots, or community)
- [ ] Are you positioning as "still figuring it out"? (Not guru)
- [ ] Is there an invitation to join the journey? (Not just consume)

**If 3+ are YES, you're building community. If 0-2, you're just posting.**
