# Subject Line Frameworks (100)

Each framework has:
- **Pattern** — the template structure
- **When to use** — the situation it fits
- **Example** — a real subject line in a direct, lowercase voice (lowercase, no em dashes, AI / social-selling / solopreneur context)

Read only the categories matched in Step 3 of the skill — don't load the whole file every run.

---

## CURIOSITY (9)

Curiosity creates an open loop the brain wants to close. Best for cold readers, educational editions, contrarian angles.

### 1. Is-It-Worth-It
**Pattern:** "is [popular thing] still worth it in [year]?"
**Use when:** A widely adopted tool/method has new alternatives or feels overhyped
**Example:** is hubspot still worth it in 2026?

### 2. Pattern Interrupt
**Pattern:** "[surprising, almost wrong-sounding statement]"
**Use when:** You have a counterintuitive take backed by real proof
**Example:** i deleted my entire crm yesterday

### 3. Surprising Success
**Pattern:** "how [unexpected person/method] [achieved big result]"
**Use when:** The success story breaks the audience's expectations
**Example:** how a 1-person business outranked a 50-person agency

### 4. Celebrity Twist
**Pattern:** "what [known figure] taught me about [your niche]"
**Use when:** You can credibly tie a known name to your topic
**Example:** what naval taught me about social selling

### 5. Hidden Knowledge
**Pattern:** "the [niche] thing nobody talks about"
**Use when:** You're surfacing something insiders know but nobody publishes
**Example:** the linkedin algorithm rule nobody talks about

### 6. Weird Question
**Pattern:** "why does [normal thing] [surprising outcome]?"
**Use when:** Audience has noticed the same weird thing but never named it
**Example:** why do my best leads come from my worst posts?

### 7. Earnings Curiosity
**Pattern:** "how i made [$X] [in timeframe] without [expected method]"
**Use when:** Source content has a real revenue number + an unconventional path
**Example:** how i made $48k in q1 without sending a single cold email

### 8. Inside Look
**Pattern:** "inside my [system/process/tool] for [outcome]"
**Use when:** You're showing the actual machinery, not the theory
**Example:** inside my 9-routine os for running a solo consultancy

### 9. High-Stakes Question
**Pattern:** "what if your [important thing] is wrong?"
**Use when:** Editing time challenges a foundational belief
**Example:** what if your icp is the reason nothing closes?

## URGENCY (5)

Urgency works only when real. Fake urgency burns the list. Best for launches, deadlines, and obsoleting old methods.

### 10. FOMO Warning
**Pattern:** "[group] is already doing [thing]. you're not."
**Use when:** A real shift is happening and the audience is behind
**Example:** every consultant on my radar is using claude code. you're not.

### 11. Time-Sensitive
**Pattern:** "[X] before [date/deadline]"
**Use when:** A real deadline exists (cohort close, price change, calendar event)
**Example:** 3 days left to fix your q2 pipeline

### 12. Obsolete Method
**Pattern:** "[thing they do] is dead. do this instead."
**Use when:** A workflow you used to teach has been beaten by a new one
**Example:** cold email is dead. do this instead.

### 13. High-Stakes Decision
**Pattern:** "the [decision] you're avoiding is costing you [outcome]"
**Use when:** Reader is dragging on a decision and the cost is invisible
**Example:** the offer call you're avoiding is costing you $5k a week

### 14. Year-Start Prep
**Pattern:** "[year]: do [X] before [Y] hits"
**Use when:** Calendar moment (year start, quarter start, season change)
**Example:** 2026: build your ai workflow before everyone else does

## PAIN POINT (11)

Name a pain the reader feels but hasn't said out loud. Best for story editions and sales-adjacent content.

### 15. Rock Bottom
**Pattern:** "the moment i [hit absolute low]"
**Use when:** You have a real low-point story that turns into a lesson
**Example:** the month i made $0 from linkedin

### 16. Pain Reveal
**Pattern:** "why your [activity] feels [bad feeling]"
**Use when:** Naming the feeling readers haven't put words to
**Example:** why your linkedin posts feel like screaming into a void

### 17. Relatable Struggle
**Pattern:** "[specific small struggle that everyone has]"
**Use when:** Universal moment that feels embarrassingly specific
**Example:** opening your dms and seeing 0 replies again

### 18. Invisible Mistake
**Pattern:** "the [thing] you're doing wrong without knowing it"
**Use when:** You're surfacing something the reader can't see in themselves
**Example:** the dm opener you're sending that kills every conversation

### 19. Hidden Mistake
**Pattern:** "i was making [mistake] for [time] before i caught it"
**Use when:** You discovered your own blind spot and want to save the reader
**Example:** i was tagging the wrong leads for 6 months before i caught it

### 20. Unspoken Reason
**Pattern:** "the real reason [common outcome] keeps happening"
**Use when:** Surface explanations are wrong and you have the deeper one
**Example:** the real reason your sales calls keep ghosting

### 21. Costly Mistake
**Pattern:** "[mistake] cost me [$X / lost time / lost client]"
**Use when:** You can attach a specific number to the lesson
**Example:** writing my own crm code cost me 3 weeks and a client

### 22. Why Everyone Struggles
**Pattern:** "why [X] is hard for everyone (including you)"
**Use when:** Reframing struggle as universal removes shame
**Example:** why follow-up is hard for everyone (including you)

### 23. Common Trap
**Pattern:** "the [niche] trap nobody warns you about"
**Use when:** A predictable pattern that catches even smart people
**Example:** the consulting trap nobody warns you about

### 24. Wishful Thinking
**Pattern:** "[hopeful belief] is killing your [outcome]"
**Use when:** A comforting belief is the actual blocker
**Example:** thinking "i'll post more next week" is killing your pipeline

### 25. Failure Stat
**Pattern:** "[X%] of [group] [fail at thing]. here's why."
**Use when:** You have a real stat that frames the lesson
**Example:** 90% of solopreneurs quit content in month 4. here's why.

## AUTHORITY (6)

Establish credibility quickly. Best when introducing a new framework or claim.

### 26. Name Drop
**Pattern:** "what [credible source] taught me about [niche]"
**Use when:** Real exposure to a known operator or book
**Example:** what hormozi's grand slam doc taught me about offers

### 27. Insider Giveaway
**Pattern:** "the [internal thing] my clients see"
**Use when:** Sharing a piece of behind-the-scenes work
**Example:** the icp matrix my clients use to score every lead

### 28. Niche Authority
**Pattern:** "after [N] [units], here's what i'd tell you"
**Use when:** You have specific volume to back the claim
**Example:** after 213 linkedin posts, here's what i'd tell you

### 29. Authority Challenge
**Pattern:** "i'm calling out [common authority claim]"
**Use when:** A widely accepted "expert" rule is wrong in your experience
**Example:** i'm calling out the "post 3x a day" advice

### 30. Niche Poll
**Pattern:** "i asked [N] [people] [question]. here's what came back."
**Use when:** Real qualitative data from your audience or network
**Example:** i asked 50 consultants what kills their pipeline. one answer dominated.

### 31. Niche Audit
**Pattern:** "i audited [N] [things]. here's what i found."
**Use when:** You did the actual work of reviewing N items
**Example:** i audited 20 consultant linkedin profiles. all 20 had the same gap.

## RESULTS (11)

Lead with the outcome. Best for case-study editions, proof-driven content.

### 32. Short-Term Win
**Pattern:** "[outcome] in [tight timeframe]"
**Use when:** Real fast result that feels almost too fast
**Example:** 3 sales calls booked in 48 hours

### 33. Instant Results
**Pattern:** "i did [X] once. [outcome] within [Y]."
**Use when:** One discrete action led to a measurable change
**Example:** i rewrote my linkedin headline once. 4 dms in a week.

### 34. Outlier Success
**Pattern:** "the [post / dm / move] that did [N×] my normal numbers"
**Use when:** A specific piece broke your usual pattern
**Example:** the post that did 12x my normal impressions

### 35. Growth Snapshot
**Pattern:** "[metric] then → [metric] now"
**Use when:** Time-stamped growth comparison
**Example:** 40 connections in 2024 → 4,800 in 2026

### 36. Top Performer
**Pattern:** "the [single thing] driving most of my [outcome]"
**Use when:** One activity does the heavy lifting
**Example:** the single dm template driving 70% of my booked calls

### 37. ROI Revelation
**Pattern:** "[$X spent] returned [$Y]. here's how."
**Use when:** Clean ROI math that surprises
**Example:** $200 in trigify spend returned $14k in pipeline

### 38. First Milestone
**Pattern:** "how i hit my first [milestone]"
**Use when:** Story of the first big number, written for people not there yet
**Example:** how i hit my first $10k month from linkedin alone

### 39. Exact Path
**Pattern:** "the exact steps from [A] to [B]"
**Use when:** A real walk-through of a specific journey
**Example:** the exact steps from cold profile view to signed proposal

### 40. High ROI
**Pattern:** "[small input] → [outsize output]"
**Use when:** Input/output ratio is genuinely lopsided
**Example:** 2 hours of content prep → 6 weeks of leads

### 41. 10x Multiplier
**Pattern:** "i [N]x'd my [metric] by doing less, not more"
**Use when:** Counterintuitive efficiency story
**Example:** i 4x'd my reply rate by sending fewer dms

### 42. Accidental Success
**Pattern:** "the post i almost didn't publish that [outcome]"
**Use when:** Story about underestimating the right move
**Example:** the post i almost didn't publish that brought in 3 calls

## CONTRARIAN (11)

Challenge a belief the audience holds. Best for thought-leadership and opinion editions.

### 43. Positive Reframe
**Pattern:** "[bad thing] is actually good for [reason]"
**Use when:** You've found upside in something the audience avoids
**Example:** getting unfollowed is the best feedback your content can give you

### 44. I Was Wrong
**Pattern:** "i was wrong about [belief]. here's what changed."
**Use when:** You publicly held a position and updated it
**Example:** i was wrong about cold outreach. here's what changed.

### 45. Dark Side
**Pattern:** "the dark side of [popular thing]"
**Use when:** Trend has hidden costs nobody discusses
**Example:** the dark side of viral linkedin posts

### 46. Hidden Cost
**Pattern:** "[free / cheap thing] is costing you [bigger thing]"
**Use when:** "Free" tool or method has a real downstream cost
**Example:** your free crm is costing you 3 closed deals a month

### 47. Unexpected Truth
**Pattern:** "[X] doesn't matter. [Y] does."
**Use when:** Audience optimizes the wrong variable
**Example:** post frequency doesn't matter. opener strength does.

### 48. Bad Advice
**Pattern:** "the worst [niche] advice i ever followed"
**Use when:** You have a personal story of bad advice gone wrong
**Example:** the worst social selling advice i ever followed

### 49. Success Despite
**Pattern:** "i [outcome] despite [conventional wisdom said no]"
**Use when:** You broke the rule and it worked anyway
**Example:** i closed 3 deals despite never sending a single follow-up

### 50. Not-What-You-Think
**Pattern:** "[topic] isn't what you think it is"
**Use when:** A widely misunderstood topic in your niche
**Example:** social selling isn't what you think it is

### 51. What-If
**Pattern:** "what if [accepted norm] is actually backward?"
**Use when:** Provocative question that opens a real debate
**Example:** what if "build in public" is actually backward?

### 52. Don't Do This
**Pattern:** "stop doing [common practice]. do [opposite] instead."
**Use when:** You can name both the bad practice and the better one (avoid the banned "Stop X / Start Y" hook in body — it's OK in subject lines)
**Example:** stop sending pitch dms. send curiosity dms.

### 53. Paradoxical
**Pattern:** "the more i [X], the less [Y]"
**Use when:** A counterintuitive inverse relationship
**Example:** the more i posted, the less my pipeline grew

## ACTION (10)

Push the reader to do something specific. Best for tactical, do-this-now editions.

### 65. Do-This-Now
**Pattern:** "do this in the next [tight time] to [outcome]"
**Use when:** A real action takes minutes and produces value fast
**Example:** do this in the next 15 minutes to fix your linkedin profile

### 66. Skip Downside
**Pattern:** "skip [common bad thing] with this [shortcut]"
**Use when:** Tactical workaround for a known pain
**Example:** skip 6 months of trial-and-error with this prospecting workflow

### 67. Stop/Start
**Pattern:** "stop [bad habit]. start [better habit]."
**Use when:** Clean before/after behavioral swap
**Example:** stop tracking impressions. start tracking dms.

### 68. Instant Upgrade
**Pattern:** "swap [X] for [Y] today"
**Use when:** Single substitution that improves outcomes
**Example:** swap your "circle back" follow-up for this one line today

### 69. Turn X Into Y
**Pattern:** "turn your [X] into [Y]"
**Use when:** Showing how to convert one asset into another
**Example:** turn your old posts into a 30-day dm calendar

### 70. Hate-to-Love
**Pattern:** "the [task] you hate that [unexpected outcome]"
**Use when:** A boring task pays off in a non-obvious way
**Example:** the dm follow-up you hate that closes 60% of your deals

### 71. Do-Or-Die
**Pattern:** "do this now or [bad outcome]"
**Use when:** Real consequence is real and time-bound
**Example:** fix your icp now or burn another quarter

### 72. Exit Strategy
**Pattern:** "how to leave [bad situation] in [timeframe]"
**Use when:** Escape route from a sticky pattern
**Example:** how to leave the "free discovery call" trap in 2 weeks

### 73. Old Way
**Pattern:** "the old way: [X]. the new way: [Y]."
**Use when:** Clear paradigm shift you can demonstrate
**Example:** the old way: 50 cold dms. the new way: 5 warm ones.

### 74. Yes-You-Can
**Pattern:** "you can [outcome] without [thing they fear they need]"
**Use when:** Reader assumes they need a thing they don't
**Example:** you can fill a pipeline without ever showing your face

**Pattern:** "[outcome] with less [X]"
**Use when:** Subtraction story (doing less, getting more)
**Example:** more dms booked with half the outreach volume

### 77. One Thing
**Pattern:** "the one thing that changed everything"
**Use when:** Genuine inflection-point story
**Example:** the one thing that turned my linkedin from invisible to inbound

### 78. Challenge Acceptance
**Pattern:** "are you ready to [bold move]?"
**Use when:** Inviting reader into a commitment
**Example:** are you ready to delete every tool you don't use?

### 79. Targeted Callout
**Pattern:** "this is for [specific reader segment]"
**Use when:** Speaking directly to one type of reader, others scroll past
**Example:** this is for the consultant stuck at 2 clients

### 80. Implied Interest

**Use when:** Conditional opener that pulls in the right reader
**Example:** if you've ever sent a dm and hated yourself for it, read this

Lead with narrative. Best for Pillar 3 (solopreneur journey) editions.

### 81. Miraculous Insight
**Pattern:** "the moment i realized [insight]"
**Use when:** Real "aha" moment with timing and place
**Example:** the moment i realized my crm was the bottleneck

### 82. High-Stakes Failure
**Pattern:** "i almost [bad outcome] before [save]"
**Use when:** Near-miss story with real stakes
**Example:** i almost lost a $15k client over a missed dm

### 83. Trial & Error
**Pattern:** "[N] versions of [thing] before one worked"
**Use when:** Showing the iteration most people skip
**Example:** 7 versions of my offer doc before one closed

### 84. Love-Hate
**Pattern:** "i love [X] and i hate [X]"
**Use when:** Honest conflicting feelings about a tool/method
**Example:** i love linkedin and i hate linkedin

### 85. Big Loss
**Pattern:** "the [significant loss] that taught me [lesson]"
**Use when:** A real loss with a clear lesson on the other side
**Example:** the $8k client i lost that taught me how to qualify

### 86. Unsuccessful Approach
**Pattern:** "i tried [popular method] for [time]. here's what happened."
**Use when:** Honest review of giving the conventional method a real shot
**Example:** i tried cold email for 3 months. here's what happened.

## TREND (4)

Tap a moving wave. Best when timing matches a real industry shift.

### 87. Tech Shift
**Pattern:** "[new tech] is changing [niche]. here's how to ride it."
**Use when:** A specific tech shift is happening NOW, not predicted
**Example:** claude code is changing solo consulting. here's how to ride it.

### 88. Breakthrough
**Pattern:** "the [tech / method] breakthrough that changes [activity]"
**Use when:** A genuine new capability you've tested
**Example:** the mcp breakthrough that changes how i prep for calls

### 89. Weird Works
**Pattern:** "[unconventional method] is working right now"
**Use when:** Something that shouldn't work, does, and you have proof
**Example:** voice notes in cold dms are working right now

### 90. Unconventional
**Pattern:** "the [unusual approach] that's beating [conventional approach]"
**Use when:** Direct head-to-head comparison
**Example:** the 2-line dm that's beating the 6-line "personalized" version

## QUICK WIN (10)

Promise a small win in a small time. Best for tactical Pillar 2 editions.

### 91. Little-Known Trick
**Pattern:** "the [niche] trick almost nobody uses"
**Use when:** A real, underused move you can demonstrate
**Example:** the linkedin profile trick almost nobody uses

### 92. Fast Win
**Pattern:** "[result] in [N] minutes"
**Use when:** Genuine fast win with a real time bound
**Example:** a sharper icp in 12 minutes

### 93. Equation
**Pattern:** "[X] + [Y] = [outcome]"
**Use when:** Two-ingredient formula that's surprisingly simple
**Example:** trigify + claude code = a pipeline you can rebuild in an afternoon

### 94. Quick Challenge
**Pattern:** "do this for [N] days. report back."
**Use when:** Inviting reader into a short experiment
**Example:** do this for 7 days. report back.

### 95. Mini Rant
**Pattern:** "small rant: [thing that needs to be said]"
**Use when:** Honest annoyance with a niche pattern
**Example:** small rant: about every "ai workflow" guru on linkedin

### 96. Overlooked Factor
**Pattern:** "the [variable] you're not optimizing"
**Use when:** Underweighted lever in the reader's process
**Example:** the headline rewrite you're not doing (and should)

### 97. Facts Over Hype
**Pattern:** "[hyped thing] vs reality: [what actually happens]"
**Use when:** Cutting through marketing claims with real data
**Example:** "ai dms" vs reality: what actually gets replies

### 98. Damn Good
**Pattern:** "a damn good [thing] for [outcome]"
**Use when:** Confident endorsement with no hedging
**Example:** a damn good dm sequence for cold connections

### 99. Insert Here
**Pattern:** "insert [thing] anywhere you have [bottleneck]"
**Use when:** Flexible solution that fits multiple slots
**Example:** insert this 4-line follow-up anywhere a deal is going cold

### 100. Cliche Disruptor
**Pattern:** "[overused phrase] is bullshit. here's what works."
**Use when:** Calling out a tired piece of advice with a real replacement
**Example:** "build in public" is bullshit. here's what works.

## How to use this file (for the model)

1. Step 3 of the skill identifies the edition intent (educational / story / authority / launch / roundup)
2. Match intent → 3-5 categories using the Edition Type table in SKILL.md
3. Read ONLY those category sections from this file (not the whole file)
4. For each subject line generated, use the **Pattern** as the structure and the **Example** as the voice/style anchor
5. Do not invent new frameworks — use one of these 100, by name
